## Decisions for KidSafe

- Prefer offline activities, large targets, visible parent gates, and clear progress.
- Give audio prompts a visual equivalent; never require a microphone or continuous connection.
- Use a parent-curated media list, not a public feed or keyword-only safety filter.
- Keep learning rewards virtual and completion idempotent.
- Separate child device privileges from the verified parent account.
- Create original stories, geometric illustrations and lesson data; do not copy proprietary artwork, audio, stories, mascots or names.
- Default advertising to off, with owner configuration and guardian consent.
- Treat repository feature claims as unverified until tested in our own implementation.
